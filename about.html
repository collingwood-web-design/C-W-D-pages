About us

We're designers, developers and strategists — a creative studio that loves to learn, collaborate and create.

Our success is not only due to the quality of our work; it's down to our attitude, our approach and the way we treat our clients.

Geek — John Warren

John was a geek before it was even trendy. After graduating he embarked on a gap year that turned into 20+ years living and working in Zermatt, Switzerland — learning timekeeping, customer care and attention to detail as only the Swiss can teach. Hooked on the web from the early days, he set up a web design and support business, then continued that work when he returned to Collingwood, Canada in 2009.

Creative — Judy Macdonald Kusoglu

Judy was born into a house of strong, creative Scottish lassies. After graduating in Fashion Design she worked for many of the best international fashion houses. She describes good design as being “a problem solver able to think outside the box.” Many miles and big brand names later, Judy brings that discerning eye to unique graphics in downtown Collingwood — just for you.
