Still have a question? →
What is AEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in traditional search results. AEO focuses on making your content clear enough that answer engines — AI Overviews, ChatGPT, voice assistants — can cite you as the answer. We treat them as one connected strategy inside the same website build.

Do I still need SEO if there's AI search now?

Yes. Strong SEO foundations — structure, content, speed, authority — are exactly what AI systems use to decide who to trust. AEO builds on SEO; it doesn't replace it.

Can AI tools like ChatGPT find my website?

They can when your site is crawlable, well structured, and clear about what you offer. Schema, helpful FAQs, and authoritative content all improve your chances of being referenced.

How long does SEO take?

Rankings usually build over weeks and months — not overnight. Results can arrive sooner. When Tropisch Tours & Travel launched, they wrote within the first week to say bookings were already coming through the site. We aim for durable foundations, not shortcuts.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No honest partner can guarantee #1 rankings. Search results change. What we guarantee is thoughtful strategy, clean execution, and a site built to earn trust — and to convert the people who find you.

Is SEO separate from web design at CWD?

No. We don't bolt search onto a finished site and hope. Structure, speed, hosting, and content decisions happen together — so visibility is part of the design, not a later upsell.
